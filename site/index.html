MacBook Pro as external display

The 16-inch MacBook Pro gets a second screen: the 14-inch MacBook Pro beside it. One USB-C dummy plug makes macOS see a real second monitor. One Thunderbolt cable carries its picture across, and a Swift app shows it full screen.

2Which port gets what

The 16-inch and the 14-inch, each seen from both sides.

3The signal path

From the dummy plug's screen on the 16-inch to the full screen on the 14-inch. All of it is in Swift.

4Step 1 needs nothing new: the CineView Nano

Before anything is bought, the Nano you own plays the dummy plug. The 16-inch's HDMI goes into the Nano, and CINEVIEW_VIEWER on the 14-inch shows the picture over the Nano's Wi-Fi. This proves the receiver first.

5The holes, and how each is closed

Two external screensThe M1 Pro drives two, which covers the dummy plus the Nano. A plain M1 drives only one, so the sender must be the M1 Pro.
The dummy's resolutionThe plug lists 2880 × 1800 and 2560 × 1600 at 60 Hz, both 16:10 like the 14-inch. A cheap plug with only 2560 × 1600 at 30 Hz was left out.
The cableThunderbolt with the lightning mark, not FireWire, and not a charging cable. The bridge appears by itself, and the two apps find each other through Bonjour.
Who "registers" the displayThe dummy registers on the 16-inch. The 14-inch only shows a full-screen window, mouse pointer included.
The Accsoon formatIt sends packets with no resend. On a cable almost nothing is lost, and one receiver then shows both the Mac and the Nano.
Never run yetCINEVIEW_VIEWER has never been compiled or run. Step 1 is its first run.
Sharpness2880 × 1800 is shown at 3024 × 1890, which is 5 % larger and nearly sharp. On the day we try BetterDisplay for the exact size.

6The order of work

  1. The Nano testThe 16-inch HDMI goes to the Nano, and CINEVIEW_VIEWER runs on the 14-inch. Nothing to buy.
  2. Buy the two partsThe dummy plug and the Thunderbolt 4 cable, from the shopping list.
  3. Mantra Display sender v1On the 16-inch: grab the dummy's screen, encode it in hardware and send it in the Accsoon format over the cable. The receiver is CINEVIEW_VIEWER.
  4. v2 and laterv2: the full native size in HEVC. v3: the 14-inch's keyboard and trackpad drive the 16-inch. v4: Wi-Fi instead of the cable.
