# LESSONS

What this project taught us, newest first.

- 21.10.2026: most cheap dummy plugs list only 16:9 modes, or 2560 × 1600 at only 30 Hz. Read the product page's resolution table, not the title. The USB-C plug B0FCDJG6SW lists 2880 × 1800 @ 60, which fits a 16:10 MacBook screen.
- 21.10.2026: no MacBook port takes a picture in. A MacBook can only be a screen through a stream, and a dummy plug gives the sender a real display to stream without a private API.
- 21.10.2026: an Amazon.de add-to-cart link (`/gp/aws/cart/add.html?ASIN.1=…`) asks for sign-in first. Marko buys from a shopping list with one link per product instead.
- 21.10.2026: Gemini (3.1 Pro, Create image, 16:9) drew labelled schematics with every label spelled right, in about 30 s each. "Download full-sized image" gives 1376 × 768 JPEG (~0.5 MB).
